Plan calculator

Know what can fit before you design.

Enter the lot dimensions and select the proposed building type. The calculator applies the setback criteria and automatically selects the corresponding Anfernee Construction plan rate.

Setback criteria

• Lots up to 100 ft deep: 15 ft front / 10 ft rear.

• Lots between 100 ft and 120 ft: 20 ft front / 10 ft rear.

• Lots above 120 ft: 25 ft front / 15 ft rear.

• Lots up to 49 ft wide: each side setback = 1/6 of lot width.

• Lots 50 ft and above: 10 ft setback on each side.
